Subscriptions

  • Cancel or change your plan: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Welty. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the period ends.
  • Restore a purchase on a new phone: in Welty, open the plans screen and tap Restore.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • One subscription covers everyone in your household space.

Your data

  • Export: Profile, Export as CSV, or Back up everything.
  • Delete your account: Profile, Delete account. This removes your account and data from our server. If you share a space, it passes to your partner.
  • What is collected and where it goes: privacy policy.

Common questions

  • Does Welty connect to my bank? No. You record money yourself, by typing, chatting with Welty or snapping a receipt.
  • Can my partner and I share? Yes. Sign in, then invite your partner from Profile. Each of you can also keep a private space.
  • Is Welty financial advice? No. Welty helps you track and plan. For decisions about investments, debt or tax, talk to a licensed adviser.
