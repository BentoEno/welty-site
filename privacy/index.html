Welty

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Welty is a personal finance app for recording money, budgeting and learning. This page says what the app collects, where it goes and how to remove it.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you record works without an account. Until you sign in, your data is kept only on your phone.
  • Until you sign in, the chat with Welty is kept only on your phone.
  • Photos you add to the chat are kept only on your phone.
  • Sample data never leaves the phone.

What is stored on our server

If you sign in to sync with a partner, these are stored on Supabase, our database provider:

  • Your account: the email address and user ID from Apple or Google sign-in, and the name you choose to show your partner.
  • Your household's money data: recorded lines, budgets, goals, accounts and balances, recurring items, debts and month-end reviews.
  • The chat with Welty: what you typed and what Welty replied, so your partner's phone shows the same conversation and it survives a new phone.
  • A count of how many AI requests you made each day, used only to cap use.
  • If you subscribe: the App Store transaction Apple signs for your subscription, used only to check that your plan is active.

Everyone in your household space can see that space's money data. Nobody else can.

What is sent to Google Gemini

Welty's assistant runs on Google Gemini. The first time you open the chat signed in, Welty asks first. Nothing is sent to Google unless you tap "Got it, continue". If you tap "Not now", nothing is sent: lines like "lunch 12" are still recorded on the phone, and Welty asks again the next time you send a question or a photo.

Once you agree, these are sent:

  • Every message you type in the chat (a line to record, a question, or a change). Welty sends your message, the last few messages of that day's conversation, your first name if you gave one, and a summary of your numbers: totals by category, your budget lines, your goals, your accounts and your money health. Other people's names and your email are not sent. Individual recorded lines are not sent.
  • Photos you add to a message (up to five), so Welty can read them.

When Welty can't be reached (no connection, or not signed in), the phone records what it can read on its own and nothing is sent. Our server does not store the photos.

Welty currently uses Google's free Gemini API tier. On that tier Google may use what is sent to provide, improve and develop its products, and human reviewers may read it. Do not send anything you would not want read.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or Apple Account details. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No connection to your bank. Welty never moves money.
  • We don't sell your data.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings, Export as CSV, or Back up everything.
  • Delete: Settings, Delete account. This removes your account and your data from our server, and, if you signed in with Apple, revokes Welty's access to your Apple sign-in. If you share a space, it passes to your partner.
  • The assistant: tap "Not now" on the chat's first card to keep everything on your phone.

Children

Welty is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here.
