Welty

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Welty is a personal finance app for recording money, budgeting and learning. This page says what the app collects, where it goes and how to remove it.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you record works without an account. Until you sign in, your data is kept only on your phone.
  • A typed line the phone can read, such as "lunch 12", is read and recorded on the phone. It is not sent to any AI service.
  • Your questions and Welty's answers in the chat are kept on your phone and are not synced.
  • Sample data never leaves the phone.

What is stored on our server

If you sign in to sync with a partner, these are stored on Supabase, our database provider:

  • Your account: the email address and user ID from Apple or Google sign-in, and the name you choose to show your partner.
  • Your household's money data: recorded lines, budgets, goals, accounts and balances, recurring items, debts and month-end reviews.
  • A count of how many AI requests you made each day, used only to cap use.

Everyone in your household space can see that space's money data. Nobody else can.

What is sent to Google Gemini

Only after you tap Allow, and only for these three things:

  • A question about your money, or a typed line the phone could not read. Welty sends your message, the last few messages of that conversation, and a summary of your numbers: totals by category, your budget lines, your goals and your money health. Names of people are not sent. Individual recorded lines are not sent.
  • A receipt photo. The photo is sent so the total can be read.
  • An Excel or CSV file. The file is sent to our server, which reads it in memory. The header and the first 8 rows are sent to Google so it can say which column is which. The rest is converted by our own code.

Our server does not store the photo or the file.

Welty currently uses Google's free Gemini API tier. On that tier Google may use what is sent to provide, improve and develop its products, and human reviewers may read it. Do not send anything you would not want read.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings, under Your data, "Answers from Google Gemini". Recording by typing keeps working without it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No connection to your bank. Welty never moves money.
  • We don't sell your data.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings, Export as CSV, or Back up everything.
  • Delete: Settings, Delete account. This removes your account and your data from our server. If you share a space, it passes to your partner.
  • Turn AI off: Settings, "Answers from Google Gemini".

Children

Welty is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here.
